# Interview Script: [Topic]

About 45 minutes: setup 5, warm-up 5, core 30, wrap-up 5.

## Setup (5 min)
- Thank them. Explain the topic broadly, without details that would bias answers.
- "There are no wrong answers. We're here to learn from your experience."
- Ask permission to record.

## Warm-up (5 min)
Broad questions about role and context. They set the baseline, so write them out:
- "What's your role at [company], and how long have you been doing it?"
- "Walk me through a typical day of [relevant activity]."

## Core questions (30 min)
Ordered from least to most specific:
1. Current workflow and general behavior
2. Pain points, friction, and specific moments
3. Needs, preferences, and specifics

For each question:
- The question, verbatim, in quotes
- *Why we're asking (for the moderator only)*
- One or two follow-ups for thin answers, e.g. "Tell me more about that." / "Why do you think that is?" / "Can you walk me through a specific time that happened?"

## Wrap-up (5 min)
- "Is there anything else you think we should know about [topic]?"
- Thank them and explain what happens next.
