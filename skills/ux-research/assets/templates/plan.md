# Research Plan: [Project or feature]

## Background
What is being built, for whom, and why research is needed now.

## Key assumptions
3–5 assumptions the research will confirm or overturn. Each reflects real uncertainty, not something the team wants confirmed.

## Research questions

### High-level (interviews)
General behavior, workflows, and mental models. Little to do with your implementation.

### Mid-level (interviews or usability tests)
Questions that touch both general workflows and your specific approach.

### Low-level (usability tests)
Whether specific design, flow, or naming decisions work.

## Methods and timeline
One short phased list: what you'll do, when, and which questions each phase answers. Interviews come before usability tests.

## Participants
Who to recruit, how many, and the spread you need (experience, use case, company size, technical depth). Don't recruit only power users.

## Interview script
The full moderator guide (see interview.md), every question written out.

## Usability test script
The full test guide (see usability.md), if a prototype exists at this stage.
