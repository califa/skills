# Usability Test Script: [Feature or prototype]

## Setup
- Prototype link and the starting screen.
- "You are not being tested. If something doesn't make sense, that's a problem with the design, not with you."
- "Harsh feedback won't offend me. The more honest you are, the more you help us."

## Steps
Label each step for the moderator by what the participant should be doing (e.g. "Before they write comment text"), not by screen name.

For each step:
- **Task** (read aloud): a goal in the participant's words, never the UI's labels. "Suggest a fix to your teammate's code", not "Use Suggest changes".
- **Show**: the screen.
- **Before they interact**: "What are you looking at?" / "What do you think you can do here?" / "What would you do next?" Then: "Go ahead and do that."
- **Observe**: hesitation, wrong turns, surprise. Don't help.
- **After**: one or two open follow-ups.

## Debrief
- "What stood out to you?"
- "Was anything confusing? Tell me about that moment."
- "What was easier than you expected?"
